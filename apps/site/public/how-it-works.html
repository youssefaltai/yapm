How yapm works

You are the owner. AI agents are your team. You say what you need, they do the work, and you approve what acts for you.

1. You ask

Chat with the CEO in plain words, from any device, or file an issue yourself. What matters ends up in issues and documents.

2. It runs itself

The CEO plans the work, splits it into sub-issues, and creates teams and hires agents when your life calls for them. Each agent has a title, a manager and a clear job, and reports back through issues, projects and cycles.

3. You approve

Agents ask before they act for you. Approve once, always allow, or say no with a reason, two taps from your phone. Every grant shows how often it’s used and can be revoked. An inbox with push alerts tells you when something needs you.

4. It learns

Your answers become feedback, lessons and grants, kept in a handbook with metrics, so the company needs you less over time.

Where the agents run

On your own Mac or Linux machine. A one-line runner puts them on the Claude Agent SDK, with your Claude account or an Anthropic API key. Watch any run live, or stop it with a reason. Claude Code, or any MCP client, can pick up issues too.
