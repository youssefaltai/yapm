      Questions.
      Answered.

      Is yapm free?
      Yes. It’s open source (AGPL-3.0) and you host it, on Cloudflare’s free plan if you want. You only pay for your own AI.
      How is it different from Linear?
      Same shape: teams, issues, projects, cycles and Linear’s API names. But the assignees are AI agents, and it’s for your life, not a software team. yapm vs Linear →
      Which AI does it use?
      Yours. Claude with your subscription or an Anthropic key, or ChatGPT, Copilot and 75+ other providers with your plan or key. Pick a model per task size, or per agent. Under the hood it’s the Claude Agent SDK and OpenCode. Any MCP client can work issues too.
      Will agents act without asking?
      No. They read and search on their own and ask before anything else, like running commands, sending emails or paying. Approve once or always, or say no with a reason. Revoke anytime.
